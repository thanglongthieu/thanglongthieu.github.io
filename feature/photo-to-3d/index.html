Image source

The image never leaves your device — it is processed in this page's canvas.

How it works

  1. The image is cover-cropped to a 768 × 768 canvas.
  2. A blurred copy is converted to luminance (Rec. 601 weights) to make a height map.
  3. The height map displaces a 256 × 256-segment plane; the photo is its colour texture.
Read the method note

Tips

  • High-contrast black-and-white photos produce crisp ridges.
  • Portraits work well with gentle smoothing (0.1–0.2) and moderate depth.
  • Dark hair and shadows read as pits — brightness is not depth.
