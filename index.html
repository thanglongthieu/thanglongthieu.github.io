03 — How the studio works

A loop, not a pipeline

  1. 01

    Ask

    Write the question down before building anything. Every experiment carries its question on its card.

  2. 02

    Build

    Prototype the smallest page that could answer it: one HTML file, no build step, works on a phone.

  3. 03

    Watch

    Use it, hand it to someone else, and log what surprises you in the Research Notebook.

  4. 04

    Write

    Turn observations into a lab note: the method, what happened, and what is still open.
